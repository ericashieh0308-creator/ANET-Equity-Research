# Arista Networks (NYSE: ANET) — Equity Research Draft

**Research date:** November 8, 2026 · **Version:** first draft · **Status:** Under Research

Amounts are **USD millions**, except EPS in USD per share and percentages.
FY means year ended December 31; Q3 means three months ended September 30;
9M means nine months ended September 30 (YTD). TTM ends September 30, 2026.
**Historical facts** below are reported figures or explicitly identified calculations;
**management statements** are issuer descriptions, explanations or expectations;
**analyst hypotheses** are unproven propositions, not forecasts or conclusions.

## 1. Investment Recommendation

**Status: Under Research.** No Buy/Hold/Sell rating, target price or fair value is
assigned. The research question is whether growth can translate into durable
cash generation at a price that compensates for concentration and competitive
risk. A completed forecast and valuation are required before answering it.

## 2. Business Overview

### Revenue model and customers

**Historical facts / reported classification:** Arista reports product and service
revenue. Management describes product revenue as switching/routing products and
related network applications; service revenue primarily reflects post-contract
support and renewals. FY2025 product and service revenue were **7,576.9** and
**1,428.8**, respectively. Service revenue should not be relabeled standalone
software subscriptions or annual recurring revenue. [10-K, Item 7, “Revenue,”
p.54; Item 8, income statement, p.69][K]

**Management statements:** Customer groups include large cloud/AI operators,
internet and service providers, AI neoclouds, enterprises and government agencies.
Arista sells directly and through distributors, resellers, systems integrators
and OEM partners. These are management's customer/channel descriptions, not
independently measured market positions. [10-K, Item 1, “Our Customers” and
“Sales and Marketing,” p.7][K]

### Products, software and competitive positioning

**Management statements:** Arista describes EOS as its network operating system,
CloudVision as its management/automation platform, and NetDL as its network data
lake. Its portfolio spans data-center/AI Ethernet switching, campus networking,
routing and support; Etherlink addresses AI networking use cases. Claims of
superior reliability or customer economics require independent validation.
[10-K, Item 1, business/products, pp.1–4; platform/competitive strengths, p.6][K]

| Competitive question | Sourced disclosure | Preliminary analyst implication, not a finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cisco networking | Arista identifies Cisco as a competitor and discusses broader competing portfolios and bundled solutions. [10-K, Item 1, “Competition,” pp.8–9][K] | Evaluate operational simplicity against procurement breadth; no Cisco/Arista price-performance ranking is established here. |
| NVIDIA networking | NVIDIA discloses Ethernet and InfiniBand platforms alongside NVLink interconnects/switches. [NVIDIA FY2026 10-K, Item 1, “Data Center,” p.5][N] | Ethernet adoption alone does not establish an Arista win. Compare like-for-like scale-up, scale-out and inter-site requirements before drawing conclusions. |
| Arista differentiation | Arista emphasizes a common software/management architecture and merchant silicon. [10-K, Item 1, competitive strengths/manufacturing, pp.6–8][K] | Test whether operational benefits support retention and pricing after competitive responses. |

These comparisons summarize issuer disclosures, not independent benchmarks.
No market-share estimate or claim of technology superiority is made.

## 3. Key Growth Drivers

### Industry and AI networking demand

**Management statements:** Arista links networking demand to AI/cloud workloads
and describes scale-up, scale-out and scale-across architectures. It also warns
that AI investment can change the mix of other spending and that trials and
acceptance terms can affect revenue timing. These statements support a research
agenda, not a market-size forecast or quantified AI revenue attribution.
[10-K, Item 1, pp.2–3; 2026 10-Q, Item 2, “Overview,” pp.14–15][K][Q]

### Preliminary investment hypotheses — not final conclusions

| Analyst hypothesis | Evidence to investigate | What would weaken it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI infrastructure investment could sustain demand for Arista's networking systems. | Customer deployment schedules and the conversion of accepted orders into repeat business; management's demand discussion above. | Delayed acceptance, spending reprioritization or competitors capturing deployments. |
| A consistent software/operations experience could support repeat purchases. | Independent customer evidence on implementation costs, renewals and purchasing decisions; the platform description in Section 2 is only a starting point. | Weak renewals, bundling pressure or little demonstrated economic differentiation. |
| Revenue growth could support operating leverage, while cash conversion remains a separate test. | Historical income and cash-flow trends in Section 4, then working-capital and investment requirements. | Gross-margin pressure or sustained working-capital absorption outweighing scale benefits. |

No growth rate, market share, AI-specific revenue amount or valuation premium is
assumed for these hypotheses.

## 5. Investment Risks

These are disclosed exposures and analyst monitoring questions, not predictions.

- **Customer concentration / hyperscaler spending:** The two largest end customers
  represented **26% and 16% of FY2025 revenue**; their identities are not inferred
  here. Arista warns that large-customer buying cycles and spending changes can
  create volatility. Monitor diversification and acceptance timing.
  [10-K, Item 1, “Our Customers,” p.7; Item 1A, pp.14–15][K]
- **Competition:** Arista discloses pricing and bundling pressure, alternative
  networking technologies and customers developing their own solutions. The
  hypothesis of durable differentiation remains unproven. [10-K, Item 1,
  “Competition,” pp.8–9; 10-Q, Part II, Item 1A, competition, pp.29–30][K][Q]
- **Margins:** Product/customer mix, component costs and pricing can change
  profitability. Monitor whether revenue growth offsets these pressures rather
  than extrapolating historical margins. [10-Q, Part I, Item 2, p.16][Q]
- **Supply chain:** Arista describes dependence on contract manufacturers and
  Broadcom switching silicon, and exposure to supplier shortages and excess
  inventory. Monitor lead times and commitments alongside actual demand.
  [10-K, Item 1, “Manufacturing,” p.8; 10-Q, Part II, Item 1A,
  supplier/purchase-commitment risks, pp.23–26][K][Q]
- **Cash conversion:** Inventory, receivables and deferred revenue affect cash
  timing. Review the operating cash-flow reconciliation rather than treating
  net income or backlog-related balances as cash. [10-Q, Part I, Item 1,
  p.5; Item 2, “Cash Flows from Operating Activities,” pp.18–19][Q]
- **Valuation — analyst risk:** Even strong operating performance need not imply
  an attractive investment at any price. Discount rates, terminal economics,
  dilution and cash conversion require justified assumptions; none are set here.

1. **NEEDS SOURCE — independent competitive evidence:** Comparable Cisco/NVIDIA/
   Arista deployment economics, performance and procurement evidence. This draft
   uses issuer descriptions, not independently verified superiority or market share.
2. **NEEDS SOURCE — AI attribution and demand durability:** Any proposed AI-specific
   revenue amount, market-size estimate or customer-spending forecast needs a
   dated primary source and a definition. None is asserted here.
3. **NEEDS SOURCE / VALIDATION — forecast and valuation inputs:** Document revenue,
   margin, tax, reinvestment and working-capital assumptions; establish the
   valuation date, market-price reference and discount-rate evidence before use.
4. **Open validation:** Reconcile annual/intangible versus interim PP&E purchases
   before TTM FCF; validate weighted-average diluted shares and rounding before
   TTM EPS. Neither limitation is resolved by this narrative draft.
5. **Coverage limit:** The annual PDF supplies 2024/2025 balance sheets, not a full
   2023 balance sheet; the interim comparison is September 2026 versus December
   2025. Obtain an earlier filing if a full three-year balance-sheet analysis is
