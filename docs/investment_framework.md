## Potential variant perception — not measured consensus

A testable analyst angle is that reported growth and low historical combined cash
purchases may overstate sustainable economic cash conversion if customer-acceptance
balances and investment requirements normalize unfavorably. The counter-case is
that scale supports operating efficiency while deferred revenue funds operations.
Neither proposition is established. No dated consensus dataset was obtained, so
this is a proposed debate, not a claim that “the market is missing” information.

## Risk assessment and review triggers

- **Concentration / hyperscaler budgets:** the two largest FY2025 end customers
  represent 26% and 16% of revenue (K p.7; Q Item 2 p.14). Monitor accepted revenue
  and customer mix; do not infer customer identities or extrapolate spending.
- **Competition / valuation:** architecture shifts, bundling and price pressure
  can weaken margins; current growth does not validate terminal economics (K
  pp.8–9; Q Item 1A pp.29–30). Reassess if differentiation lacks customer evidence.
- **Margin / supply chain:** product mix, merchant silicon, contract manufacturers,
  inventory and commitments link demand to cash risk (K p.8; Q pp.9–10,16,23–26).
  Do not count purchase commitments as both debt and forecast operating spending.
- **Cash conversion:** deferred product revenue and deferred cost of goods sold
  require matched economic classification (Q Note 3 p.9). Reassess assumptions
  if inventory/deferred-cost absorption persists without cash conversion.
- **Model / dilution:** unresolved reserve, tax/claims, awards, NWC, CapEx and
  WACC/terminal inputs prevent a defensible target. A price quote is not a cure.

No numerical thesis stop-loss, catalyst date or price-based recommendation is
invented. The next decision is whether evidence can support the missing economic
inputs. Until then, retain explicit scenario uncertainty and NOT RATED status.
